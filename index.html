Welcome to Ethiopia

Eleven days, one team, one celebration

The Lightyear leadership group (LYL) travels to Ethiopia alongside Imagine1day to learn, lead, and celebrate together. The team gathers in Addis Ababa, trains at Bishoftu, then journeys south to Hawassa and Dilla to inaugurate the Konga School — closing with the Great Ethiopian Run back in the capital.

Tap a hotel for its website, or a travel leg for directions. Times are a planning guide and may shift on the ground — your team leads will confirm details each day.
